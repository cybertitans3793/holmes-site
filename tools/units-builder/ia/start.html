Rules: groups and academic honesty

Working in a group

Groups of up to three can share a setup and help each other collect data, but:

  • Each person needs their own research question: a different IV, or the same IV and a different DV.
  • No two people may hand in the same raw data.
  • Each person writes their own report. Group reports are not allowed.
  • Name your group members when you submit Stage 1.

Academic honesty

  • AI tools are not allowed for any part of the IA (FCPS policy).
  • Cite every source you use: websites, textbooks and class notes.
  • Your report is checked against your earlier stages and with plagiarism software.
  • Asking for help is expected and never penalised. Ask early.

Other things that are allowed

  • A simulation or database instead of a hands-on experiment, but it needs deeper analysis (for example, getting a physical constant from it).
  • SL and HL use the same task and the same criteria.

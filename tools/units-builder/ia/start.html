What the IA is

The internal assessment (IA) is one experiment you design, run and write up yourself. You choose a question, collect your own data, analyse it and write a report of at most 3,000 words.

~10 hof class time in all
  • Your data must be numbers (quantitative). Add written observations where they help.
  • Most IAs in this class are hands-on experiments. A simulation or database is allowed, but it needs deeper analysis (for example, getting a physical constant from it), not just a graph of numbers.
  • Your topic does not have to be original. Ideas from books, websites or the topic list are fine. The data, analysis and writing must be yours.
  • SL and HL use the same task and the same criteria.

Working in a group

You may work in a group of up to three. You can share the setup and help each other collect data, but:

  • Each person needs their own research question, with a different IV, or the same IV and a different DV.
  • No two people may hand in the same raw data.
  • Each person writes their own report: their own analysis, conclusion and evaluation. Group reports are not allowed.
  • Tell Mr. Holmes who is in your group when you turn in Stage 1.

Academic honesty

  • Cite every source you use: websites, textbooks, class notes, and any tool that helped you, including AI tools.
  • Your report is checked against your earlier stages and with plagiarism software.
  • Asking for help is expected and is never penalised. Ask early.

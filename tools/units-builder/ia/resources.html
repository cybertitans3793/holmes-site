Useful tools

  • Phone sensor apps (for example phyphox, free): stopwatch triggered by sound, accelerometer, light meter, magnetometer, frequency analyser.
  • Slow-motion video on your phone, for fast motion: count frames to get times.
  • Google Sheets for tables, calculations and graphs; Google Drawings for diagrams.
  • The gradient tool on this page, to check your gradient and its uncertainty.

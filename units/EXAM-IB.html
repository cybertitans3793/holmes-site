Command terms

These are the command terms in the IB Physics guide. They tell you how much depth the answer needs, and the marks tell you how many points to make.

TermWhat it asks for
Knowledge (AO1)
DrawAn accurate, labelled diagram or graph in pencil. Use a ruler for straight lines, draw diagrams to scale, plot points correctly and join them with a straight line or smooth curve.
StateA specific name, value or other short answer, with no explanation or calculation.
Applying (AO2)
AnnotateAdd short notes to a diagram or graph.
CalculateA numerical answer, showing the relevant stages of working.
DescribeA detailed account.
EstimateAn approximate value.
OutlineA brief account or summary.
Analysing and evaluating (AO3)
AnalyseBreak something down to bring out its essential parts or structure.
DetermineThe only possible answer.
DiscussA considered, balanced review covering a range of arguments, factors or hypotheses, with opinions or conclusions stated clearly and supported by evidence.
ExplainA detailed account that includes reasons or causes.
PredictAn expected result.
Show (“show that”)The steps of a calculation or derivation.
SketchA diagram or graph, labelled as needed, giving the general shape or relationship and including the relevant features.
SuggestA possible solution, hypothesis or other answer.
Also used in the IA criteria
IdentifyAn answer chosen from a number of possibilities.
JustifyValid reasons or evidence that support an answer or conclusion.

The guide says other words may still appear in questions (for example deduce or evaluate). They are not defined in the 2025 glossary, so read them in their plain meaning.

Source: IB Diploma Programme Physics guide, first assessment 2025: “Glossary of command terms”, pp. 76–77, and the IA command terms table, p. 71. Assessment details: pp. 62, 64 and 68.
